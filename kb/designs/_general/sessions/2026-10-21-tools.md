Built a fully parametric 2 mm GT2 pulley (Shooter → Tools → "GT2 Pulley (parametric)") from one execute_code script, scripts/fusion/gt2_pulley.py. Groove fitted to goBILDA's STEPs. Added 0.1 mm tip fillets, removed the set screw and hub, and added an optional Sonic Hub mount group.

## Tool lessons
- Parametric builds need execute_code: our tools pass createByReal numbers and circular_pattern copies bodies. Use ValueInput.createByString(expr) and set dimension.parameter.expression; a feature pattern quantity of 'Teeth' sticks.
- Grooves can vanish silently after a big Teeth drop: a point-on-OD-circle constraint flipped to the far side and the cut missed. Place flank ends by a sqrt() dimension instead, and count real groove faces rather than trusting timeline health.
- Fusion expression min()/max() separate arguments with ';' not ','.
- execute_code has a 30 s limit and the result is lost even when the script finishes. Fillets slow rebuilds (0.5 s at 20T, 1.7 s at 60T), so build in one call and change one parameter per call.
- Dimensioning every hole center from the origin over-constrained the sketch at hole 2. Dimension hole 1, then make the others equal and aligned to it.
- Pick profiles by centroid, not with 'is'. TimelineGroup.isSuppressed works from the API, so a timeline group serves as an on/off toggle without Configurations.
- Measure COTS geometry from vendor STEPs: import them into a throwaway document and section them. Wrap getStrokes per edge in try/except.
- Tip fillets fail when a tooth's flat top is narrower than 2 × TipR (60T profile plus clearance). Keep the default at 0.1 mm.

## Decisions and why
- Default groove = goBILDA 20–40T (depth 0.746, width 1.23, R 0.54) — droftarts' V was ~0.2 mm wider (backlash); 60T Viper groove 0.82/1.385/0.62 is in the parameter comments.
- Clearance default 0.15 mm — parts are always printed.
- Bore = 8 mm circle ∩ 7 mm hex (REX) — covers round or hex by changing two numbers.
- No set screw or hub; 9 mm long with a 6 mm belt — user request.
- Sonic Hub mount (16 mm pattern M4, 14.3 × 2.2 boss pocket) suppressed by default — holes cut through below ~50T.

## Rejected and why
- Configurations for pitch switching — not on the personal plan; revisit with the education license.

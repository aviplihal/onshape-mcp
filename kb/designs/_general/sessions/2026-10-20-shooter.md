Added two live-axle counter rollers to the 'Shooter Parts' hood from a constrained master sketch, driven from the pivot axle, then split it into Main Body / Hood sub-assemblies with a working hood joint.

## Tool lessons
- A document that opens with 0 bodies may have its timeline marker rolled back (here step 2 of 50) — check get_timeline before calling it empty.
- Ping answers but document commands time out — Fusion's main thread is blocked (open dialog or command panel); ask the user to press Esc.
- Sketch on the XZ plane: sketch Y = −world Z — negate Z when drawing from world coordinates. draw_arc sweeps in degrees, counter-clockwise in sketch space.
- Meshing COTS gears overlap in CAD until phased: rotate one by half a tooth (4.5° for a 40T) — tooth overlap fell from 18.8 to 0.37 mm³.
- Find a joint's real range by stepping it with interference checks — the hood's pose was the bottom of travel, not the middle.
- goBILDA STEPs arrive off-origin in arbitrary orientations — read each part's axis and extents before placing; a 20T pulley went in backwards.

## Decisions and why
- Two counter rollers form the back of the ball path, 14.6 mm apart (7.7°) — user wanted the whole back to be rollers with only a small angle.
- Roller = 8 mm REX live axle + printed sleeve Ø10.7 (REX + 0.3 clearance + 2×1.2 wall) + McMaster 5234K51 tubing (12% stretch) → Ø13.5 — smallest size with a printable wall.
- Drive: 40T on the flywheel/pivot axle → 20T idler on the arm, 60T→20T GT2 belt around both rollers, 6.0× (+12.5% surface speed) — gear mesh and belt length stay constant as the hood pivots.
- Extra flywheel mass goes on a roller axle: Ø26×4.4 mm steel ≈18 g matches a 115 g flywheel — stored energy scales with ω², and the roller spins 6×.
- Hood: Axon servo → Sonic Hub → REX → 15T MOD2 pinion on a 122T sector (8.13:1); range 0 to −10° — +5° hits the base plate.

## Rejected and why
- Rollers fixed in the side plates — compression would change with hood angle.
- Reverse gear on the frame — belt length to the hood would change as it pivots.
- Belt straight between the rollers — 14.6 mm centers need a ~35T belt that doesn't exist.

## Open questions
- 14.6 mm roller spacing clashes with Ø15 bearing flanges and Ø20.5 pulley barrels — spread to ≥21 mm or use slim printed pulleys?
- Arm bearings stick 1 mm out of a 3 mm arm into a 0.5 mm arm-to-plate gap — 4 mm arm or a spacer?
- Idler shaft is supported on one end only — needs an outer plate.
- Roller-to-flywheel speed ratio: decide by the gear (36T/40T) × tubing-wall test plan.

Fixed real clashes in the user's Shooter Parts (roller pockets 0.4 mm apart, arm bearings into side plate, cantilevered idler) by rebuilding the hood drive from a parametric master sketch: 4 mm printed arm, outer plate, printed GT2 pulleys, steel inertia ring.

## Tool lessons
- extrude direction=negative was symmetric (setDistanceExtent's 1st arg is isSymmetric); fixed in the add-in. Check body thickness with get_bounding_box after every extrude.
- Profile order is unpredictable (hole disc vs annulus); after extrude check list_faces for the concave hole and undo if wrong.
- Default body names (Body1..) repeat across components; use the 'Occurrence/bN' path with rename_body, boolean_operation, get_bounding_box.
- Deleting or re-inserting an occurrence that came from Cut/Paste brings the original back at root; sweep root afterwards into an Obsolete component.
- create_component children aren't ground-to-parent and stay put when the parent's joint moves; add a rigid as-built joint, then sweep_joint scoped to the subassembly.
- Old rigid groups block move_occurrence; no tool deletes them, suppress_feature works.
- export_step fails for bodies; model printed pulleys as parametric flange-OD stand-ins.

## Decisions and why
- Roller pitch 14.6 -> 16.2 mm, squeeze kept (40.02 mm from ball centre) — 2 mm wall between 14.2 pockets.
- Printed 59T idler + 20T roller pulleys replace 3421+Hyper Hub and 3422 — 264 mm belt still fits, ratio 5.9x, ~2.5 cm shorter stack.
- Inertia for <=5% dip per shot: I_eq = 23 * m_ball * r_fw^2, independent of speed; 25 g POLLEN gives 190 g*cm^2 on the 6x roller axle.
- Steel ring 56/43 x 4 mm (32 g, 198 g*cm^2) on a printed PETG hub, outboard of the outer plate.
- Printed 4 mm arm, 0.5 mm from side plate; 4 mm outer plate with two dia 10 columns (M4 inserts) inside the belt loop.
- POLLEN = 25 g (AndyMark 0.055 lb).

## Rejected and why
- Ring inboard — any disc around roller 1 hits roller 2's axle or pulley.
- Mass on the idler axle — only 2x flywheel speed, needs ~9x the mass.

## Open questions
- One motor at 4800 rpm recovers a 9.2 J shot in ~0.4 s (~2.5 balls/s); 4/s needs two motors or gearing near half free speed.
- Rollers ~28,300 rpm: check 1611 bearing rating and ring balance.
- Servo-side pivot bearing still overlaps the side-plate bearing; Hood Arm R is still 3 mm.

Overnight solo pass on Shooter Parts. audit_shaft_stacks found the user's planted faults (flipped flywheel Hyper Hub, R1 axle short with no bearing in Hood Arm R); fixed them, added spacers and REX set-screw collars from real KB stacks, moved the servo pinion off the Axon case, spoked the 94T. Audit clean, only intended contacts left, hood sweep −10..0 has only CAD artifacts.

## Tool lessons
- Interference never finds missing parts: run audit_shaft_stacks after every shaft change; it caught a flipped hub and a short axle that 'clean' interference reports hid all session.
- Moving nested occurrences in this parametric doc is unreliable: re-inserts compose transforms (T⁻¹·M), copies of one component share child placements, and deleting the old copy shifts the new one. Do ONE relative move from a freshly opened doc, verify, stop.
- To shift a single-use part, use a Move feature on its body inside its own component (moveFeatures, defineAsFreeMove): lasting and timeline-safe. Its vector is in assembly (world) axes, not component axes.
- When placement goes wrong twice, close without saving and reopen the last saved version instead of patching; save_document before every risky step.
- The hood joint is the as-built 'Hood angle' (limits −10..0), not 'Revolute 8'; list joints with occurrences before sweeping.
- Raw add-in calls accept a top-level "timeout" (≤120 s); execute_code otherwise cancels at 30 s.

## Decisions and why
- Hyper Hub pilot into Rhino 14 mm bore, flange on the web, clamp collar toward Side Plate L — matches Droid Force flywheel stack.
- Close every axial gap with printed Ø10/8.2 spacers on bearing inner races — top-team stacks (Droid Force, RoboNoodles) never leave gaps.
- One 2920 set-screw REX collar per roller shaft, staggered (R1 arm-L side, R2 block side) — every part on REX was slip-fit, nothing located the shafts.
- Added R1 bearing in Hood Arm R and extended the axle 9.5 mm to y 8.40 like R2 — the seat existed but was empty.
- Servo pinion moved 3.6 mm out along the spline: it was sunk 3.45 mm into the Axon case; 4 mm spline engagement kept.
- 94T spoked (6×5 mm): 10.9→5.5 cm³.

## Rejected and why
- Rebuilding the Flywheel stack rigid group around repeated occurrence moves — caused drift; reverted to saved version instead.
- Inserting the 2920 STEP into a nested roller stack — landed at y≈−12; modelled the collar as a Ø15.3×8.5 named body.

## Open questions

- Did the user plant more faults than the hub, the R1 bearing and the R1 axle? The audit only sees 3 shafts (not pins/idler shafts).
